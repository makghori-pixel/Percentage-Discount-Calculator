How to Use the Percentage & Discount Calculator

Our online percentage calculator is designed to provide quick and accurate calculations for everyday shopping, financial budgeting, and math problems. Whether you are attempting to determine sale prices at retail stores or compute percentage growth metrics, this tool handles the math automatically in real-time.

Key Features & Modes

  • Discount Off Mode: Ideal for shopping trips. Simply enter the original sticker price along with the store discount percentage to discover your final total and overall dollar savings.
  • Basic Percentage Mode: Quickly calculate precise fractional amounts (e.g., finding a 15% tip on a restaurant bill).
  • Percentage Change Mode: Calculate the percentage increase or decrease between two numerical values over time.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do you calculate a discount percentage manually?

To calculate a discount by hand, multiply the original price by the discount decimal (percentage divided by 100), then subtract that number from the original price.

Is my calculation data saved or sent to a server?

No. All calculations run strictly client-side inside your internet browser using vanilla JavaScript. No financial inputs or personal information are stored or transmitted anywhere.
