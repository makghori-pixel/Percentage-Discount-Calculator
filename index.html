Fast, Interactive Percentage & Discount Calculations

Our online percentage calculator helps shoppers, business owners, and students calculate price markdowns, sale savings, and percentage growth instantly. The tool runs 100% locally in your browser for privacy and speed.

How to Calculate Discounts Manually

To compute a retail discount by hand:

  • Divide the percentage discount rate by 100 to get a decimal (e.g., 20% / 100 = 0.20).
  • Multiply the original item price by the decimal to find your total dollar savings.
  • Subtract the total dollar savings from the original price to find the final sale price.
